The series

Down 3–1 after Game 4, Cleveland won three straight to take the title. The Cavaliers won Game 5 in Oakland 112–97 and Game 6 at home 115–101, forcing a winner-takes-all Game 7 back at Oracle Arena.

Game 7 stayed inside four points for its final six minutes. With 1:50 left and Cleveland up by one, LeBron James chased down Andre Iguodala for the block; 47 seconds later, Kyrie Irving hit a three over Stephen Curry to make it 92–89. The Cavaliers finished 93–89 — the lowest combined-scoring Game 7 of the decade.

James averaged 29.7 points, 11.3 rebounds and 8.9 assists across the seven games and recorded a triple-double (27–11–11) in the clincher. Irving added 26 points in Game 7. It remains the only 3–1 comeback in NBA Finals history.
