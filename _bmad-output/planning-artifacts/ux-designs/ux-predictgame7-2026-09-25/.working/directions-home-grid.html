UX session · Story 6.3 · 2026-10-21 · design directions

Home's first screen: three grid layouts

Same content, three layouts for the photo-editorial Game 7 grid. Every tile links to its series page and carries no visible caption; its accessible name (aria-label) holds the series name. Each direction shows state 1 (offseason) and state 2 (Game 7 pending, SIMULATED with the 2027 Eastern Conference Finals, Celtics vs Knicks) on desktop and phone. State 3 (playoffs on, nothing pending) is a one-line strip, shown once at the end for all three. Photos are CSS stand-ins; the dashed boxes are mock notes, never shipped.

A. BentoB. Mosaic wallC. FilmstripState 3 strip

ABento

Bento gives the five flagships a hierarchy: one Game 7 at poster scale and four supporting shots, all on the first screen at 1280 with no scrolling. It is the most magazine-cover of the three and the most predictable to load, since the grid never grows beyond five or six images with exactly one large. The cost is that only flagships fit; the rest of the archive sits behind one text link.

Personality: a magazine cover. Curated, still, one hero and its supporting cast.

BMosaic wall

The mosaic wall treats Home as the front door to the whole archive: an even, dense, edge-to-edge field of Game 7s where the volume is itself the message (after HTML5 UP Multiverse). It is the only direction that needs designed tiles at scale, so it tests whether crest-and-year tiles can sit beside photos without reading as placeholders. It has the highest image cost (about eight photos on the first screen) and the weakest hierarchy until a Game 7 goes live and claims the 2×2 slot.

Personality: an archive wall. Dense, even, browsing over reading.

CFilmstrip

The filmstrip is cinematic: one wide lead frame owns the first screen and the rest of the story waits in a sideways reel beneath it. It gives the pending state the clearest single focal point and the cheapest first paint (one large image; strip tiles lazy-load as they scroll in). Its risk is discoverability, since tiles hidden to the side get fewer taps, so the peek, the edge fade and the track bar all have to work.

Personality: a film premiere. One frame at a time, the reel waiting below.

State 1 · Offseason

The lead is the most recent flagship; the other four plus a "Browse every Game 7" end card ride the strip. The browse link also sits as text above the strip.
