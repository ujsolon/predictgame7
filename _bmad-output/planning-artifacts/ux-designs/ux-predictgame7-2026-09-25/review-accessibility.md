# Accessibility Review — PredictGame7 UX Spines

Ad-hoc AA review for the Finalize gate. Scope: DESIGN.md, EXPERIENCE.md, and the three 1:1 mocks (OG card, series page, error/empty states) in `ux-predictgame7-2026-09-25/`. Ratified existing surfaces, gated features, and the light-only decision are out of scope per instruction. Contrast ratios computed with WCAG 2.1 relative-luminance formula.

## Overall verdict

The spines have an unusually strong behavioral accessibility floor (aria-live strategy, accessible names, role="status" wiring, touch-target intent are all explicitly spec'd), but the color layer ships at least three guaranteed AA failures as written: the retry panel's muted-foreground-on-muted pair fails even after the decided `#767676` token fix, the `destructive #DC3C3C` inline-error text lands at 4.42:1 (just under the 4.5:1 floor), and the 1:1 mocks hardcode `#808080` for meaningful 12–18px text everywhere — directly contradicting the spines' own interim restriction. The video facade's iframe swap lacks a title/focus-handoff spec, which is the main keyboard-path gap. All findings are fixable with token- and spec-level edits before build; none require rethinking the surfaces.

## Findings

- **critical** — Retry panel description text fails AA with no compliant path as spec'd. `muted-foreground` on the panel's `muted #F5F5F5` fill is 3.62:1 at `#808080` and still only **4.17:1 at the decided `#767676`** — below 4.5:1 for 14px text. The owner's token-tightening decision does not rescue this pair because the failure comes from the fill, not the text token alone. (location: DESIGN.md · Components · retry-panel + frontmatter `retry-panel.background: {colors.muted}`; mockups/key-error-states.html · `.retry-panel p { color:#808080 }` on `#F5F5F5`). *Fix:* spec the retry-panel body copy to render on white (panel becomes bordered-white with muted icon tile only), or darken the panel text token for muted fills (e.g. `#595959`, ~7:1 on #F5F5F5), and state the pair explicitly in DESIGN.md so builders don't derive it from the two independent tokens.
- **high** — `destructive #DC3C3C` as inline-error text is **4.42:1 on white** — fails AA for the spec'd `text-sm` (14px) error copy on every new/changed form (Predict custom, Contact rebuild). Passes 3:1, so the error *border* is fine; the *text* is not. (location: DESIGN.md frontmatter `colors.destructive` + Components · inline-field-error; EXPERIENCE.md · Accessibility Floor "all new type meets AA contrast"; mockups/key-error-states.html · `.field-error`). *Fix:* add a darker error-text token (e.g. `#C22F2F` ≈ 5.5:1 or `#B91C1C`) reserved for small-text destructive use, keeping `#DC3C3C` for borders/large text only; note it beside the `#767676` decision.
- **high** — The 1:1 mocks hardcode `#808080` for meaningful small text throughout (12px eyebrows/labels, 12px `score-sub`, 14px rail/nav items, 16px `cta-line`, 18px `hero-standfirst`, 404 body, empty-state line, video-credit link), contradicting the spines' own interim rule that `#808080` is restricted to ≥24px text and decorative use until the token tightens. A builder treating the mocks as the build spec (their stated purpose) ships 3.95:1 small text on every new surface. (location: mockups/key-series-page.html · `.label`, `.hero-standfirst`, `.score-sub`, `.cta-line`, `.notfound p`; mockups/key-error-states.html · `.retry-panel p`, `.empty-state p`; vs EXPERIENCE.md · Accessibility Floor). *Fix:* swap all meaningful small-text instances in the three mocks to `#767676` (or add a top-of-file comment in each mock: "all `#808080` small text renders as `#767676` per owner decision 2026-09-25"), so the mock and the floor agree.
- **high** — Video facade → iframe swap has no accessible-name or focus-handoff spec. The spines say the iframe "swaps in on tap" but never require a `title` attribute on the iframe (4.1.2 name for the embedded player region), and never say where focus goes when the activated play button is removed from the DOM — focus silently resets to document top, stranding keyboard users away from the player controls they just triggered. (location: EXPERIENCE.md · Component Patterns · Video embed + Interaction Primitives "Tap video facade"; DESIGN.md · Components · Editorial content; mockups/key-series-page.html · `.video-facade`). *Fix:* add to Accessibility Floor: "the swapped-in iframe carries `title='{video title} — via {creator}'`, and focus moves to the iframe on swap so player controls are the next tab stop."
- **high** — Resting form-control borders are `#E6E6E6` (**1.25:1 vs white**), failing 1.4.11's 3:1 minimum for the visual information needed to identify a control's boundary — and the forms carrying them (Predict custom, Contact rebuild) are explicitly in-scope new/changed surfaces. (location: mockups/key-error-states.html · `.select-box`, `.field-input` (non-error state); DESIGN.md frontmatter `border: '#E6E6E6'` with no form-control carve-out). *Fix:* spec a form-control border token ≥3:1 on white (e.g. `#949494` ≈ 3.0:1 or `#767676`) in DESIGN.md · Components · inline-field-error / form patterns; keep `#E6E6E6` for decorative hairlines and card borders.
- **medium** — Touch-target spec vs mock disagree, and several new-surface targets have no ≥44px spec at all. The spine requires ≥44×44px hit areas for the icon-only Share and facade play control, but the mock's icon Share button is 36×36 with no hit-area expansion, the outline Share (~37px tall), `.btn-primary` CTA (~40px), and the empty-state/404 onward links (bare inline text, ~26px) are unspecified — all are primary mobile targets on surfaces NFR-U1 covers. Facade play control (72px) and similar-series rows (full-width, ~61px tall) are fine. (location: EXPERIENCE.md · Interaction Primitives touch-target line; mockups/key-error-states.html · `.icon-ghost-btn`, `.btn-outline`, `.empty-state a`; key-series-page.html · `.btn-primary`, `.notfound a`). *Fix:* generalize the touch-target rule to "all interactive targets on new surfaces ≥44×44px hit area (visual may be smaller; expand via padding/pseudo-element)" and update the mock button paddings to match.
- **medium** — `success #22C35D` (2.32:1), `warning #F59F0A` (2.13:1), and `info #1A80E6` (3.96:1) on white are reserved for "data states (series status, pipeline indicators)" with zero usage constraints — as text, as dots, or as icons, all three fail 4.5:1 and two fail even the 3:1 large-text/graphics floor. The retry panel's warning icon on the accent tile (`#F59F0A` on `#F0F0F0`, **1.87:1**) is already an instance. Two compliant builders will produce different outcomes (colored status text vs dot+ink label). (location: DESIGN.md · Colors "Semantic" bullet + frontmatter tokens; mockups/key-error-states.html · `.warning-tile`). *Fix:* add a rule: semantic state colors are never used as text and never as the sole carrier of meaning — status renders as an ink text label optionally accompanied by a ≥3:1-on-its-fill colored dot/icon; state colors as pure decoration are exempt. Either darken the warning icon (e.g. `#B45309`) or mark the retry-panel icon `aria-hidden` decorative (the adjacent heading already carries the meaning).
- **medium** — Facade activation ambiguity: EXPERIENCE says "Tap video facade — swaps to the YouTube iframe" while the mock implements a discrete centered `<button>`. A builder following the spine could make the whole 16:9 facade a click-target div — visually identical, keyboard-inaccessible. (location: EXPERIENCE.md · Interaction Primitives; mockups/key-series-page.html · `.video-facade`). *Fix:* one line in Component Patterns · Video embed: "the labeled play button is the only activation control; the facade surface itself is not clickable."
- **medium** — Focus management is unspecified when content regions are replaced: the retry panel "replaces the failed region in place" and the form-success confirmation "replaces the form region" — if the user's focus is inside the replaced region (e.g. on the submit button that triggered the failure), focus drops to `<body>`. `role="status"` handles announcement but not focus position; "Retry is the first tab stop inside it" only helps a user who is already tabbing from a sane position. (location: EXPERIENCE.md · Component Patterns · Retry panel / Form success confirmation; Accessibility Floor). *Fix:* spec: "when a region is replaced, focus moves to the replacement container (or its heading, `tabindex='-1'`); Retry is the first tab stop from there."
- **low** — OG card eyebrow `#808080` on the `#1A1A1A` field is **4.41:1 at 14px** — below 4.5:1. The card is a PNG consumed off-platform (outside the app's WCAG surface, and the spines correctly make it decorative to AT), but the pair is a DESIGN.md frontmatter token (`og-card.eyebrow`) that a builder could reuse for any future in-app ink-field rendering, and 14px text scales down badly in feed crops. (location: DESIGN.md frontmatter `components.og-card.eyebrow`; mockups/key-og-card.html · `.og-eyebrow`). *Fix:* bump the on-ink eyebrow to ≥`#8A8A8A` (~5.2:1 on #1A1A1A) in the og-card token, or annotate the token "image-only; not an in-app text pair."
- **low** — Select control value text color is ambiguous: the mock renders unselected/placeholder state ("Select a team") and the preserved-input values in `#808080`/`#262626` inconsistently across `.field-input` vs `.select-box`. Placeholder text is a WCAG gray zone; a *chosen value* rendered at 3.95:1 is a straight failure. (location: mockups/key-error-states.html · `.field-input { color:#808080 }`). *Fix:* spec that selected values always render in ink (`foreground`), and placeholder in the tightened `#767676`.
- **low** — SPA 404: heading level and route-change announcement unspecified. The mock uses `<h2>` (mock-frame artifact); the real fallback page needs an `<h1>`, and since unknown ids are reached via client routing as well as cold loads, the document `<title>` must update (and ideally the 404 heading receive focus) so screen-reader users get the state change — react-router does not do this automatically. (location: EXPERIENCE.md · State Patterns "Unknown series id"; mockups/key-series-page.html · `.notfound`). *Fix:* add "404 page: `<h1>` headline, `<title>` set to the 404 copy, focus moved to the headline" to State Patterns.
- **low** — Facade thumbnail alt policy unspecified. The spine lists "thumbnail" among the prerendered facade elements but never says whether it's an `<img>` with alt, `alt=""` decorative, or a CSS background. (location: DESIGN.md · Components · Editorial content; EXPERIENCE.md · Component Patterns · Video embed; absent from key-series-page.html). *Fix:* spec thumbnail as `<img alt="">` (decorative — the labeled play button and visible credit carry the meaning); keeps the no-JS crawl requirement without duplicating the video title to AT.
- **low** — Video-credit link's non-color affordance is a `#E6E6E6` bottom border (1.25:1) in `#808080` text with `text-decoration:none` — link identification relies on a nearly invisible cue. (location: mockups/key-series-page.html · `.video-credit a`). *Fix:* render the credit link in the tightened `#767676` with a real `underline` (or border ≥3:1), and confirm the standard focus-visible ring applies.

## Verified clean

- **Ink-on-white core type:** `#262626`/`#1A1A1A` on `#FFFFFF` = 15.1:1 / 17.4:1 — passes everything.
- **Decided token fix is sound:** `#767676` on white = **4.54:1** — clears 4.5:1 for small text (the fix just needs to be applied consistently per the findings above).
- **OG card type on ink:** `#FAFAFA` on `#1A1A1A` = 16.7:1; tagline `#D4D4D4` = 11.7:1; "VS" at 72% opacity blends to ≈9.1:1 at 26px — all pass (only the eyebrow flagged).
- **Share a11y path:** accessible name "Share this series" spec'd for the icon-only form; "Link copied." fires in the sonner region already wired `aria-live`; clipboard-blocked fallback toast spec'd — no dead end, no silent failure.
- **Retry/success announcement strategy:** retry panel `role="status"`, Retry first tab stop, inputs preserved; form-success confirmation `role="status"` — coherent aria-live strategy across all three async surfaces.
- **Inline error wiring:** `aria-describedby` + `aria-invalid` present in the mock, error announced with the field, no toast/alert double-announcement.
- **Facade play control (as mocked):** real `<button type="button">` with descriptive `aria-label`, 72px hit area, decorative SVG `aria-hidden`.
- **Alt-text policy:** editorial images carry required alt text (DESIGN.md); in-app OG-card renderings describe the matchup, not the design; card is decorative to AT with the link carrying meaning; team logos `alt=""` inside the labeled card composition; decorative arrows/chevrons/icons `aria-hidden` throughout the mocks.
- **prefers-reduced-motion:** series-page fade/slide explicitly disabled; the existing motion vocabulary is already gated app-wide per Foundation.
- **Focus order / structure on the series page:** single longform column, one `<h1>`, sections `aria-labelledby`-wired in the mock, natural DOM order sidebar → main → CTA; similar-series rows are full-width links well over 44px tall.
- **Guardrails intact:** no prediction outputs on custom-matchup cards; credit always visible as text (never burned into the frame); no hover-only affordances; no autoplay without a tap.
