### Diff-scope review: write path (`bmad-review`, three lenses)

**W1: A rejected write after a winner-filling write skips the insights refresh, and the error gives no recovery hint.** *Fix now.*
- **Where:** `run.ts:389-438`. The refresh-failure branch (`:426-435`) carries the `--refresh-insights` recovery. The write loops have no such branch: any throw lands in the generic catch at `:440`.

- **Untested:** the test `FakeSink` has no completion-failure hook. It has only `failNextBirth` and `failNextRefresh` (`tests/pipeline/run.test.ts:70-74`). Grep for `failNextComplete` and `failComplete` across `tests/pipeline` finds nothing.
